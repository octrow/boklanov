Based on the novel of the same name by Ernest Hemingway.

Having lost the miracle fish, left without oars and harpoon, having suffered a crushing defeat, the Old Man found something very important in this journey, as director Rinat Kiyakov says about the play.

An iconic role in our story is played by the boy Manolin. He is the little "anchor" who holds the boat with the Old Man and keeps it from perishing, from disappearing into the endless waves.
