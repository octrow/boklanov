    Эта история о семилетнем мальчике Никите, которого родители отправили в деревню к двоюродной бабушке на все лето. Никита ещё никогда не жил вдали от дома без мамы и папы.   en: |-
    This story is about a seven-year-old boy, Nikita, who is sent by his parents to the countryside to stay with his great-aunt for the whole summer. Nikita has never lived far from home without his mother and father. He has never been to a store or washed dishes. He will have to learn a lot, but how is all of this done? He's still so small!.. Grandmother will help him with this.

    Based on the book by Daria Vardenburg.
