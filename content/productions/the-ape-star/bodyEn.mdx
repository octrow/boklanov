A nine-year-old girl named Yunna was adopted by a large black gorilla with shaggy paws and crooked teeth. Yunna dreamed
of having a different mother - one who was beautiful and smelled good. However, she had to figure out what to do now
that she had been adopted by this gorilla. This orphanage story turns into a series of adventures in a performance by
Roman Boklanov, and at the end, it becomes clear that appearance or other "ideal" circumstances are not the most
important thing when it comes to relationships.

The play is filled with childish mischief, music, and lots of objects - these, made in different scales, convey the wide
range of emotions of the characters as they find their way to each other.

Based on the book by Frida Nielson.
