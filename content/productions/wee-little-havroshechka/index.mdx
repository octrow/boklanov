    Знакомую всем историю о судьбе девочки, которая осталась сиротой и попала в дом к злой мачехе, в спектакле рассказывает сама Хаврошечка.   en: |-
    The familiar story of the fate of a girl who becomes an orphan and ends up in the house of her wicked stepmother is told by Khavroshechka herself in the play. She tenderly remembers her happy childhood, her mother and father. It is difficult for the girl to live in a strange family, and only the cow Ryabushka, inherited from her by magical powers, helps her.

    Khavroshechka overcomes all the difficulties that come her way and retains her kind heart. And she is rewarded for it.

    This is a sad tale with a happy ending, which teaches kindness, hard work, and faith in miracles.
