# Online

“ONLINE” is a documentary play for teenagers. It tells about life on the internet, and this story is more relevant now than ever. The play is based on real events, interviews, and materials taken from open internet sources.

![photo_2022-10-29_20-48-11.jpg](Online%208e33-5472/photo_2022-10-29_20-48-11.jpg)

Page of the performance: [[in the theatre]](https://barnaul.icity.life/afisha/teatr/17336-online)

Duration: 1 hour 20 minutes

Age category: 14+

**Premiere:** March 27, 2020

**Director:** Roman Boklanov

**Artist:** Vera Petrovskaya

**Playwright:** Katya Sverdlova
