A forest village is bustling with activity. The inhabitants are preparing for the New Year and waiting for the traditional holiday cake. After all, what is a New Year without a New Year's treat? But this time the holiday may not come! On New Year's Eve, all the pies mysteriously disappear.

Are the forest dwellers doomed to be left without a holiday? Or will there be a brave hero who will solve this mystery and return all the pies to the homes of the inconsolable forest dwellers?
