  en: |-
    Some argue that the story of Ayaccio, the Aladdin circus clown, is only a legend.

    But it doesn't matter how fictional the story is; what matters is how beautiful it is.

    This is a play about a great love coming to a friendly circus family, and with it, the fear of losing it. This tender story will touch the heart. And who knows, perhaps it will stay there forever.

    ***Author:** Biagio Russo*
